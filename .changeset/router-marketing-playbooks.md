The gooseworks router (2.2.0) routes marketing work to the 14 marketing playbooks: where-to-start, research-market, research-competitors, research-audience, write-outreach, plan-campaign, make-static-ads, run-paid-campaigns, analyze-performance, email-lifecycle, improve-search-visibility, work-with-creators, make-content and run-organic-social. They replace the older Brand Growth skills in its table (one job and one playbook per row). New static image ads go to make-static-ads; goose-ads now covers changing one ad already made in GooseWorks and saving the customer's image as a template. The router's description says "find leads and contacts" instead of promising enrichment, and "anything else" now means finding a skill or a single step with catalog search.

Fetches of the playbooks succeed once the goose-skills catalog publishes them.
