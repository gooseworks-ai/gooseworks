`video make --detach` prints exactly one JSON line on stdout (its other words go to stderr), and two detaches of the same video never start two runs. Every way a make can fail, including one before the run starts, ends on a result with its stable exit code, and a sign-in refusal from the line now exits with 6 from every path. The run's time limit counts from the start of the make, and the process ends within 30 seconds of a stop. A finished video is saved as `final.mp4` only once our server passes it, and `video verify` names a final only after a run that ended done. Taking a step out again repairs a damaged untouched copy, edited copies are stored one at a time, and an edit's diff is cut to the request's byte budget.
