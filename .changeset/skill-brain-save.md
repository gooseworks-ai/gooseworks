Entry skills read the Marketing Brain first and save brand facts to it. The gooseworks router (2.1.0) starts brand work with brain_map, uses brain_open and brain_search for details, and saves any lasting fact the customer states about their brand (an offer and its dates, a price, a "never say X" rule, their audience, a bestseller, a correction) with brain_save in their words, even when they only asked for copy. Facts never go to the agent's own memory, and brand_update stays for products, the kit and setup. Its description now also loads it when the customer talks about their GooseWorks brand's offers, prices, rules or audience or asks for captions and other copy. goose-ads (3.1.0) and goose-product-photos (1.1.0) carry the same save rule, and their rules blocks move to the server's rulebook 1.5.3, which starts brand work with brain_map.

Release with the matching server Brand Brain tools (gooseworks-app #2104, #2107); don't promote to main until they are in production.
