Entry skills read the Brand Brain first and save brand facts to it. The gooseworks router (2.1.0) starts brand work with brain_map, uses brain_open and brain_search for details, and saves any fact the customer states about their brand (an offer and its dates, a price, a "never say X" rule, their audience, a bestseller, a correction) with brain_save in their words, even when they only asked for copy. Facts never go to the agent's own memory, and brand_update stays for products, the kit and setup. Its description now also loads it when the customer talks about their brand's offers, prices, rules or audience or asks for captions and other copy. goose-ads (3.1.0) and goose-product-photos (1.1.0) carry the same short rule.
