Custom videos now measure the exact final before upload and stop with plain time ranges when it fails, instead of sending it. The measurements allow silence or a held picture in the first 1.5 s and last 3.0 s, a 2.0 s mid-video freeze and a steady sub-bass line, so real ads with end cards and bass lines are not stopped. Content reviews state how audio and motion were checked (listened or measured), match severities case-insensitively, block on unknown severities and name any unknown field by path. Assembly timelines use whole milliseconds and pixels, drop local paths before upload, support crop and scale with limits on stretch and zoom, cap pauses and freezes and allow deliberate `callback:` repeats. Linked timelines now reach every part that declares one, and html-frames 1.3.1 widens its duration tolerance to one output frame. When a custom video waits on a review or a fix, the kit tells the run before it stops. Template videos no longer warn about audio review when no speech is planned. The make-custom-video entry text matches the current server text. Release with the matching server support for `final_measure`, the new review capabilities and the `final_measure_v1` feature.
